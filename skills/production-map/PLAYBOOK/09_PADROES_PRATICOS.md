# 09 — PADRÕES PRÁTICOS

## 1. Referência de cor sem copiar personagem

```text
The supplied portrait controls only the exact hair color inside the reflection. Preserve the canonical character’s face, age, haircut and wardrobe. Do not transfer the reference person, clothing, background, text or graphic elements.
```

Use quando uma referência deve controlar uma única variável.

## 2. Isolamento espacial de efeito

```text
The physical character always has black hair. Red hair exists only inside the clearly bounded reflective surface. The mirror edge is an absolute color boundary.
```

Repita a regra positiva e a negativa quando a ferramenta insiste em contaminar o mundo real.

## 3. Contagem anatômica

```text
The creature has exactly six attached load-bearing legs: a front pair beneath the chest, a middle pair beneath the central ribcage and a rear pair beneath the pelvis. All attachment points are visible.
```

Transforme número abstrato em estrutura verificável.

## 4. Escala

```text
The creature’s shoulder height is approximately four times the child’s height. Even in deep background, its back rises above the vegetation and its horns occupy the upper third of the composition.
```

Defina escala relativa e presença no quadro.

## 5. Movimento físico de câmera

Fraco:

`The camera opens to reveal the museum.`

Forte:

```text
The pursuit rig decelerates while the vehicle maintains speed. Increasing physical distance allows the car to recede as the operator performs one coordinated pan and outward arc, revealing the museum without a cut or digital zoom.
```

## 6. Acting contido

```text
Her eyes register the reflection. Her pace changes by less than a quarter step. She takes one small breath. Curiosity becomes a private smile while she continues walking.
```

Dirija tempo, corpo e transição emocional.

## 7. Transformação com frente legível

```text
Green begins exactly beneath her shoe. Fine veins race across the ivory surface, thicken into roots and leaves, and the camera accelerates beside the leading edge of the growth.
```

Defina origem, direção, matéria e câmera.

## 8. Estado base travado

```text
Edit the supplied approved environment while preserving the exact architecture, camera, crop, lens perspective, floor plan and lighting direction. This is a new state of the same space, not a redesign.
```

## 9. Continuação a partir de frame real

```text
Begin exactly from @frm_07. Preserve the notebook position, hand placement, camera height, walking motion and page geometry. Do not reconstruct a different angle.
```

## 10. Círculos separados

```text
Each circle appears fully formed in a previously empty area. No circle grows out of another, moves, touches, merges or overlaps. Preserve visible paper between every circle.
```

## 11. Som sem música

```text
No music and no score. Use only diegetic sound: footsteps, fabric, paper, room tone, wind and material contact.
```

## 12. Revisão que preserva acertos

```text
Preserve the approved photography, acting, camera movement and environment. Correct only the physical scale of the creature and the visibility of the middle leg pair.
```

## 13. Insert de cinco segundos

Use quando a sequência principal funciona, mas falta uma ponte:

```text
Create one five-second continuous insert beginning from @frm_start and ending in a frame compatible with @frm_end. The camera remains close to the protagonist; the environment changes during one complete orbit.
```

## 14. Três finais alternativos

Trave o mesmo começo e varie somente a assinatura:

- final por reflexo;
- final por luz;
- final por composição aérea;
- final por som;
- final por match cut.

Registre cada alternativa sem substituir a anterior antes da montagem.

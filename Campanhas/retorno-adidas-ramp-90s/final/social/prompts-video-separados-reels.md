## 🎬 CENA 01 (00:00 - 00:07) — "O Batismo da Lixa"
* **Duração Recomendada**: 5 a 7 segundos  
* **Movimento de Câmera**: Câmera macro estática no nível do chão com micro-vibrações táteis e aproximação lenta (*Slow Macro Push-in*).

### Opção A: Image-to-Video (I2V) — *Recomendada*
> *Use a imagem gerada pelo prompt da Cena 01 como input inicial.*
```text
Slow macro camera push-in. The vintage navy blue suede Adidas skate shoe is being aggressively scraped back and forth across the coarse black skateboard grip tape. Small particles of white concrete dust and shredded suede fibers fly off from the heavy friction. Harsh direct camera flash lighting casting deep static shadows. Subtle natural handheld micro-jitter, 35mm film grain, 24fps documentary skate footage, hyper-realistic physical friction and texture.
```

### Opção B: Text-to-Video (T2V) — *Geração Direta por Texto*
```text
Vertical 9:16 extreme macro shot at ground level. A brand-new chunky 1990s navy blue suede Adidas skate sneaker with thick white leather stripes and fat laces is rubbing violently against coarse black skateboard sandpaper grip tape. Real friction motion, white dust flying off the textured suede, rough scuff marks appearing on the toe box. Hard direct on-camera flash illumination in darkness, intense film grain, raw 1990s skate videotape aesthetic, slow forward push-in, 24fps, photorealistic.
```

* **Controles de Câmera (Runway / Kling)**:
  * `Motion`: 4 / 10 (movimento contido focado no calçado)
  * `Camera`: Zoom In (lento), Pan = 0, Tilt = 0
  * `Frame Rate`: 24 fps

## 🎬 CENA 02 (00:07 - 00:15) — "O Impacto da Aterrissagem"
* **Duração Recomendada**: 7 a 8 segundos  
* **Movimento de Câmera**: Ângulo rasante em lente olho de peixe extrema com trepidação dramática no choque contra o piso (*Fisheye Ground Tracking + Impact Camera Shake + Quick Refocus*).

### Opção A: Image-to-Video (I2V) — *Recomendada*
> *Use a imagem gerada pelo prompt da Cena 02 como input inicial.*
```text
Extreme fisheye lens action. The skateboarder lands hard from the air onto the rough urban concrete stairs, stomping the chunky navy blue Adidas Ramp sneakers down with explosive force. The gum rubber soles compress and flex on impact, kicking up a small cloud of urban dust. Dramatic violent camera shake at the exact moment of landing, followed by a fast tilt-down refocusing directly on the sneakers resting firmly on the pavement. 1996 Sony VX1000 fisheye aesthetic, harsh strobe lighting, gritty night atmosphere, 24fps.
```

### Opção B: Text-to-Video (T2V) — *Geração Direta por Texto*
```text
Vertical 9:16 ground-level ultra wide fisheye perspective with circular barrel distortion. A street skateboarder flies into frame and stomps an aggressive heavy landing down city marble stairs at night. The feet wearing chunky 1990s navy blue and white Adidas Ramp sneakers slam onto the concrete. Heavy camera shake on the violent impact, concrete dust rises. The camera quickly tilts down and zooms into the scuffed sneakers on the ground under a single streetlamp. Century Optics Mk1 fisheye lens distortion, authentic 1990s VHS skate video look, raw motion, 24fps.
```

* **Controles de Câmera (Runway / Kling)**:
  * `Motion`: 7 / 10 (alta dinâmica de ação e impacto)
  * `Camera`: Shake / Jitter no impacto, Tilt Down no final
  * `Frame Rate`: 24 fps

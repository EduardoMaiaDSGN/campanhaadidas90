# Prompts Completos de Geração de Imagens — Campanha Adidas Ramp 90s

> Todos os prompts foram calibrados com direção fotográfica analógica noventista: iluminação de flash direto, granulação de filme 35mm, lentes clássicas de skate (Fisheye Century Mk1 / Sony VX1000) e texturas táteis (lixa, concreto, cera e camurça pesada), evitando o aspecto de render 3D plástico.

---

### PROMPT 01: Key Visual Master (Cartaz Oficial com Lettering)
- **Função**: Imagem-mãe da campanha, anúncios principais e pôster de lançamento.
- **Aspect Ratio**: `--ar 4:5` (e `--ar 9:16` para Stories/Reels)
- **Engine Recomendada**: `gpt_image_2` (via Higgsfield CLI) ou Midjourney v6
- **Prompt**:
```text
An authentic 1990s underground skateboard magazine print ad and street poster for the rerelease of the classic Adidas Ramp sneaker. Bold brutalist composition inspired by 1990s DIY punk skate fanzines and Ray Gun magazine layouts. In the center, a rugged low-angle shot of the chunky 1990s Adidas Ramp skate sneaker in rich navy-blue suede with bold white leather three stripes, puffy stuffed tongue, fat cotton laces, and a durable gum rubber cupsole, resting on a chipped urban concrete ledge dusted with skate wax and grip tape scuffs. Raw direct on-camera flash photography, harsh deep shadows, authentic 35mm film grain, slightly weathered paper texture with visible photocopy halftones, tape strips on the corners, and subtle photocopied fold marks. Across the top in heavy distressed black-and-white xerox-style typography, the headline reads: 'FEITO PRA LIXA.' In the lower right corner, a vintage Adidas Trefoil heritage skateboarding logo with a weathered red rubber stamp that says 'OFFICIAL 90s RE-ISSUE'. Atmospheric, gritty, uncompromising street culture aesthetic, cinematic realism, shot on 35mm film. --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 6.1
```
